Inside the formula

Three ingredients you'll hear a lot about.

We'd rather explain a few ingredients properly than list fifty. Here's what sits at the centre of the bare routine, and why.

The signature botanical

Madagascar Centella Asiatica

A plant long used in skincare for its soothing, comforting feel. We source ours from Madagascar and put it in every bare formula — it's the “soft” in SoftScience.

The tone balancer

Niacinamide

One of the best-studied skincare actives, known for helping skin look more even and less oily. Too much can sting some skins, so we buffer it with centella.

The barrier builder

Ceramide complex

Ceramides are lipids naturally found in the skin's outer layer. Topped up in a cream, they help skin hold on to moisture and feel comfortable again.

Questions

Good to know.

When and where can I buy bare?

bare launches through partner dermatology and aesthetic clinics in Kerala in 2027, then online later that year. Join the list and we'll tell you as soon as a clinic near you stocks it.

Why clinics first?

Because the best person to recommend a skincare routine is someone who has actually looked at your skin. Starting in clinics also means our formulas get honest feedback from professionals before they reach everyone else.

Is bare suitable for sensitive skin?

Every formula is designed with reactive, easily irritated skin in mind. Everyone's skin is different, though — patch test any new product first, and if you have a skin condition, ask your dermatologist.

Is bare a medicine?

No. bare products are cosmetics. They're designed to care for, soothe and protect the skin — not to diagnose, treat or cure any condition. For that, please see a doctor.

What is SoftScience?

It's our formulation rule: every proven active is paired with a calming botanical — in our case, Centella Asiatica from Madagascar — so you get results without the fight.

Is haircare coming too?

Yes. A bare haircare line built on the same SoftScience approach will follow the skincare launch, through the same partner clinics.
